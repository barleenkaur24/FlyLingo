# 🎤 FlyLingo — 90-Second Winning Pitch Script

**Japan Airlines Hackathon Problem Statement 2 (FlyLingo)**  
*Total Time: 90 Seconds | Team of 4 Members*

---

### [00:00 – 00:20] The Problem Hook (Person 4 – Presentation Lead)
> **Person 4 (Holding crew tablet):**  
> *"Good afternoon, respected judges. Japan Airlines is globally renowned for Omotenashi—the highest standard of hospitality. But on long-haul flights from India to Tokyo, critical communication breaks down. Passengers who do not speak Japanese or fluent English struggle to express dietary restrictions or sudden medical sickness. And at 35,000 feet, cloud translation apps fail completely because aircraft have zero internet.  
> Today, our team presents **FlyLingo**: 100% offline, privacy-first, and built for real aviation safety."*

---

### [00:20 – 00:35] Offline Edge Proof (Person 2 – Backend & Network Engineer)
> **Person 2 (Showing server & network monitor):**  
> *"Judges, please observe: our laptop and smartphone are connected to a local air-gapped cabin Wi-Fi hotspot. Cellular data and internet are 100% turned off. Zero bytes of expensive satellite bandwidth are consumed.  
> Our backend auto-detects the local aircraft IP, generates a dynamic QR code on the boarding pass, and enforces an air-gapped zero-knowledge privacy firewall that protects passenger PII from crew and vice versa."*

---

### [00:35 – 00:50] The Passenger Journey & Gating (Person 1 – Frontend Engineer)
> **Person 1 (Holding smartphone to camera/judges):**  
> *"When the passenger boards, they scan the QR code on their boarding pass. Their ID is generated automatically from their name and birthdate.  
> Notice our systematic flight gating: during the 10-second boarding and the Boeing 787 altitude climb to 35,000 ft, service buttons are strictly locked for flight safety compliance. Once cruising altitude is reached, the cabin chime sounds and services unlock."*

---

### [00:50 – 01:10] Voice AI & Keigo Translation (Person 3 – AI & Speech Engineer)
> **Person 3 (Speaking into smartphone microphone in Hindi):**  
> *(Taps mic)*: *"Mujhe bahut tez sar dard hai aur chakkar aa raha hai."* *(Severe headache and dizziness)*  
> *"Our offline NLP categorizes this in under 2 milliseconds without any cloud API, detects a high-priority medical alert, and translates it into polite Japanese Keigo: 『激しい頭痛とめまいを訴えていらっしゃいます。』"*

---

### [01:10 – 01:30] Priority Queue Escalation & Closed Loop (Person 4 – Crew Console)
> **Person 4 (Turns tablet toward judges):**  
> *"Instantly, on the crew tablet, Seat 24K flashes Crimson Red! Because it is a medical alert, our Priority Queue Array preempts all other requests and moves Seat 24K to Index #0.  
> The attendant taps **'対応中 (Resolving)'** to begin care. Once delivered, the passenger taps **'Resolved'** on their phone, completing the two-way closed loop.  
> FlyLingo delivers authentic Japanese hospitality at 35,000 feet—completely offline, secure, and accessible to everyone. Thank you."*
